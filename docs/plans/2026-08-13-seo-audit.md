## MEDIUM

### M1 — Open Graph image is a 512×512 logo

`layout.tsx` `openGraph.images` → `/logo.png` 512×512. Social cards expect ~1200×630. Will look like a small logo on WhatsApp/Facebook.

**Fix:** Add `src/app/opengraph-image.tsx` (or `public/og.jpg` 1200×630) and point metadata at it. Keep logo as apple-touch.

### M2 — Age claim mismatch in meta vs programs

`siteConfig.description` and home meta: **0–6 yaş**. Programs/FAQ: **18–48 ay**. Not a robots issue; mixed SERP snippet vs on-page programs.

**Fix:** Do **not** change marketing copy to a new age without the client. Optional: meta description “18 aydan itibaren” only if they confirm. Leave as MEDIUM / by-design unless told.
